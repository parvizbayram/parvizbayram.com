|Brand Identity

The Baku Museum of Miniature Books is the world’s only museum dedicated to miniature books. It was listed in the Guinness Book of Records for having the world’s largest collection of such books. The museum houses books of various types and sizes, starting from 10x10 mm.

ROLE

BRAND DESIGNER

The branding was designed for the world’s only museum of miniature books. Baku Museum of Miniature Books was listed in the Guinness Book of Records as the largest collection of miniature books in the world. There are different types and sizes of books starting from 100x100 mm to 10x10 mm in the museum.

Considering the variety and non-standard sizes of these miniature books, we have designed a unique logo that shows these properties. In the logo, the books seem to be laid out on top of each other, and its adaptability lies in the fact that these “books” can be laid out next to each other too.

The ratio of the rectangle sizes is equal to the Golden Ratio.
