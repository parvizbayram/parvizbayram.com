Manaw

|Brand Identity

Manaw is an online supermarket based in the Netherlands. It offers a wide range of products and delivers them to the door of its enormous amount of active clients. Their main difference from the other similar services in the country is that they make products from all over the world easily accessible to their clients. Apart from that, despite being fully online service, they deliver the groceries in less than 21 hours after accepting an order.

We were asked to do brand restyling for them and create set of graphic assets to represent their company image on various platforms. Therefore, we are gladly presenting their updated color scheme representing the types of their products, visual guideline to use on social media, the set of icons and illustrations matching their branding to be used on digital products.
