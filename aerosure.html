Aerosure

|UX/UI Design

Aerosure company is a reliable partner in the supply of aviation and petrochemical products that meet generally accepted global quality standards.

ROLE

UX/UI DESIGNER

While undertaking this project, my primary objective was to establish Aerosure as a globally recognizable brand by designing a promotional website that embodies distinctive aesthetics and unique elements. Additionally, the client requested the consolidation of all company services onto a single, easily navigable page, thereby enhancing their clarity and accessibility. To accomplish this, I incorporated 3D models for each section, imparting a distinctive character to each.

Moreover, in order to maintain transparency in the presentation of services, I employed a judicious combination of white space and subtle light gradients.
