Hey! I am Parviz

Design found me before I had a name for it -
first as a kid navigating a PC at three,
then teaching myself Photoshop around eight,
and earning my first money from design at thirteen.

That independent path gave me
a real understanding of how design works
in practice, long before I could properly explain it.

Over eleven years of professional work,
I've gained experience across
a wide range of areas

→ Logo & brand identity

→ UX/UI

→ Information architecture

→ vibe coding

→ no-code web development

→ Visual Strategy

→ Art Direction

→ Visual Communication

→ Campaign design

→ Advertising design

→ Digital DESIGN

→ Motion design

→ 3D (Cinema4D / Redshift)

At advertising agencies, I grew from designer to
art director, worked on TV commercial productions,
and collaborated with clients including
the United Nations, SEIKO, USAID, Unibank and
Pasha Bank, which are among the leading banks
in their country. During that time I also mentored
designers at different stages of their careers
and took on talent management responsibilities.

Later, I co-founded a boutique design studio
- a tight two-person creative collaboration -
that worked with clients from more than
20 countries, including Red Hat and Airalo,
and was listed among the top branding studios
on Dribbble. My work has been featured on
Behance and published on Ads of the World
by Clio Awards. Alongside studio work, I've spoken
at Friends of Figma and have been shortlisted at
Jolbors Festival and recognised at the Felis Awards.

Eleven years of practice teach you one

design works best when usefulness and
aesthetics hold equal weight, and its ideas
are always felt before they are understood.
