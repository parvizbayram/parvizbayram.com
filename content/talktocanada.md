# TalktoCanada

Project type: UX/UI Design

Role: UX/UI Designer

Canonical URL: https://parvizbayram.com/talktocanada

TalkToCanada is an English school in Canada that focuses on improving accents. Its programs pair students with native-speaking tutors for live, personalized conversation practice designed to build both fluency and confidence.

We worked with TalkToCanada, an English school in Canada, to design a website that showcases their specialized accent reduction program for all adults. Our focus was to create a user-friendly and visually appealing platform that clearly communicates the program's value and drives engagement.

In addition to designing a modern interface optimized for both desktop and mobile users, we also crafted custom illustrations and icons to enhance the visual storytelling.
